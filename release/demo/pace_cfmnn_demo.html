Explanation (PACE-SHAP)

The values share out the predicted class's margin over the runner-up, s′A − max s′c, against its average over the training rows. With two features the Shapley values are exact: each is the average of its two marginal contributions.

The rules follow the pace_cfmnn package. The ramp is f(t) = max(0, 1 − max(0, γ·min(1, t))), with membership the mean of [f(x − w) + f(v − x)] / 2. Boxes within 10⁻⁹ of the top membership are tied, and the nearest centroid wins. A repair moves the face of the winning wrong-class box nearer the error just past it (by 10⁻³), on the feature where that cuts the smallest share of the box's width. The gate rejects the edit if training macro-F1 falls by more than 0.5 points or any class's recall by more than 1. The certificate is the tight one: points where the edited box is in the tied top group before or after the edit, and the top groups hold more than one class.

This page is a separate JavaScript version, written for illustration. The reference is the Python package. The demo tries each current error once per round, for up to 10 rounds; the package spreads 300 tries over 10 rounds.
